# Business Questions and Insights

Use these questions to explain the project in interviews. Add your actual query results and observations after validating them in PostgreSQL and Power BI.

1. **Inventory valuation:** What is the estimated value of stock currently held?
   - Metric: Total Inventory Value
   - Business use: Understand the amount of working capital tied up in inventory.

2. **Low-stock monitoring:** Which products are below their reorder point?
   - Metric: Low Stock Products
   - Business use: Prioritise replenishment and reduce the risk of stockouts.

3. **Category analysis:** Which product categories account for the largest share of inventory value?
   - Visual: Inventory Value by Product Category
   - Business use: Compare how inventory value is distributed across categories.

4. **Replenishment planning:** How much should be ordered to bring stock up to target stock?
   - Metric: Suggested Reorder Quantity
   - Business use: Create a starting point for replenishment review.

5. **Supplier performance:** Which suppliers have the highest on-time delivery percentage?
   - Metric: On-Time Delivery %
   - Business use: Support supplier performance discussions.

6. **Procurement overview:** What is the total procurement spend and how many purchase orders are represented?
   - Metrics: Total Procurement Spend and Total Purchase Orders
   - Business use: Monitor purchasing activity.
